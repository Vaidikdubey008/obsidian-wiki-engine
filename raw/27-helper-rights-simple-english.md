# Module 27: Helper Rights (Simple English)

---

## Overview

This module is written for **helpers**, not employers. It uses simple English (Grade 6 reading level) so helpers from any source country can read and understand. The chatbot should serve this content directly to any helper user, optionally translated (see Module 03 for language switching).

This module covers your basic rights as a domestic helper in Singapore. **You should know these. They protect you.**

---

## 27.1 Your Salary

### What you get
- The salary in your contract — every month
- Paid in full
- Paid by the date in the contract (or within 7 days)

### What is OK
- Salary paid by bank transfer or cash
- You sign a paper to say you got it
- Your employer can keep a copy of the salary record

### What is NOT OK
- Employer pays late more than once
- Employer pays less than the contract
- Employer takes money for breakages, food, or anything not in the contract
- Employer says "I will pay you later" many times

### What to do if salary is not paid
1. Tell your employer once, calmly
2. If still not paid → WhatsApp Ming Hwee at **+65 8011 9456**
3. We will help you talk to your employer
4. If still not paid → MOM can help (1800-339-5505). MOM helps helpers get unpaid salary back. This is one of the main things they do.

## 27.1a Your Placement Loan (Very Important — Read This)

Many helpers pay an agency in their home country to get the job. Often you do not pay cash — instead, money is taken from your salary for the first months. This is called a **placement loan** or **agency loan**.

**This is the biggest money question in your first year. You have the right to understand it fully.**

### What you should know and be able to check

You should be able to answer all of these. If you cannot, ask us.

1. **How much do I owe in total?**
2. **Who do I owe it to?** (The agency in my country? Ming Hwee? A finance company?)
3. **How much is taken each month?**
4. **How many months in total?**
5. **What paper did I sign?** (You have the right to a copy. Ask for it.)
6. **How much have I paid so far, and how much is left?**

### Your rights

- You have the right to a **copy of the loan paper you signed**, in a language you understand
- You have the right to know your **balance at any time** — just ask
- The amount taken each month **cannot be more than what you agreed and signed**
- After the loan is finished, your **full salary must start again immediately**
- The loan is **separate from your rights**. Owing money does NOT mean you must accept bad treatment, no rest day, or no food. Nobody can say "you still owe money, so you cannot complain."

### If you are not sure

Message Ming Hwee: **+65 8011 9456**. Say: *"Please tell me my loan amount, how much I have paid, and how much is left."* We will tell you.

If you think too much is being taken, or you were told a different amount before you came — tell us. We will check it and explain it to you. If you are still not satisfied after we have explained, tell us that too — we would rather sort it out with you than have you worrying about it.

The chatbot must not guess at any of these numbers. Until this is filled in and verified, the bot answers loan questions by routing to a human.

## 27.2 Your Rest Day

### What you get

There are TWO rules. Many employers only know the first one. You should know both.

**Rule 1 — One full day off, every week.**
You can agree to work that day for extra money instead. That is your choice.

**Rule 2 — At least ONE day off every month that must be a REAL day off.**
This one cannot be changed to money. Not even if you ask for money. You must actually take the day off.

This rule started in January 2023. It is the law. Your employer can be punished if they do not follow it.

### Rest day means
- You do NOT work that day
- You can leave the house
- You can do whatever you want (within the law and your contract)
- You come back by the time agreed with your employer (usually night, before sleep)

### What is OK
- You and your employer agree which day (most take Sunday)
- You and your employer can agree on extra money instead of the day off — but ONLY if you both agree, and ONLY for the extra days, not for your one real day off each month
- The extra money is the same as one day's salary
- Your one real day off each month can be one full day, or two half-days
- If you cannot take it this month, you must take it before the end of next month

### What is NOT OK
- Employer says "no off-day for you"
- Employer keeps cancelling your off-day
- Employer pays you less than one day's salary in lieu
- Employer pays you money every week and you never get a real day off — **this is against the law, even if you agreed to it**

### Why the one real day off matters

The government made this rule so you can rest properly and **meet people outside the house**. If something goes wrong at work, the friends you make on your day off are the people who can help you. That is why your employer cannot buy this day from you.

### What to do if off-day is denied
1. Talk to your employer first — say "I would like my off-day on [day]"
2. If denied → WhatsApp Ming Hwee at **+65 8011 9456**
3. We will help you negotiate

## 27.3 Your Passport

### Your right
**Your passport belongs to YOU. You should keep it.**

### What is OK
- You keep your passport in your room or your locker
- You and your employer agree to keep it in a household safe — but you must know the combination or have a key
- You can take it out anytime

### What is NOT OK
- Employer keeps your passport and you cannot get it
- Employer says "I will give it back when contract is over"
- Employer hides it from you

### What to do if passport is taken
1. Ask politely — "Please can I have my passport"
2. If refused → WhatsApp Ming Hwee at **+65 8011 9456**
3. This is against Singapore law — we will help

## 27.4 Your Food

### What you get
- 3 meals a day
- Enough food
- Food that you can eat (if you don't eat pork, no pork; if you don't eat beef, no beef)

### What is OK
- You eat the same food as the family, OR
- You cook your own food, OR
- You and the family agree on a food plan

### What is NOT OK
- You are hungry every day
- You are given only leftovers
- You are not allowed to eat the food you cook
- You lose weight because of not enough food

### What to do
1. Tell your employer — "I am still hungry, can I have more rice"
2. If you are hungry every day → WhatsApp Ming Hwee at **+65 8011 9456**

## 27.5 Your Room and Sleep

### What you get
- A place to sleep
- A mattress, pillow, blanket
- A place to put your things
- Privacy when you change clothes
- At least 7 hours of sleep at night

### What is OK
- You sleep in a small room
- You share a room with female family members
- You sleep in a part of the home that has privacy (curtain, partition)

### What is NOT OK
- You sleep on the floor with no mattress
- You sleep in the kitchen or bathroom
- You sleep with a male family member (not in the same bed or room)
- You are woken up many times every night

### What to do
1. Talk to your employer
2. If not solved → WhatsApp Ming Hwee at **+65 8011 9456**

## 27.6 Your Phone

### Your right
You can have your own phone. You can talk to your family.

### What is OK
- Employer says "no phone during work" — that is fair
- Employer says "no phone after [time] at night because baby sleeping" — that is fair
- You agree on phone time

### What is NOT OK
- Employer takes your phone
- Employer says "no phone ever"
- Employer reads your messages without permission
- Employer says you cannot call your family

### What to do
1. Talk to your employer
2. If your phone is taken → WhatsApp Ming Hwee at **+65 8011 9456**

## 27.8 Things You Should NEVER Do

### These can hurt you very seriously

- **Do not hit or hurt the children.** This is a serious crime. You can go to jail.
- **Do not steal from the family.** This is a serious crime. You can go to jail and be sent home with no payment.
- **Do not lie about big things.** Small mistakes are okay — tell the truth.
- **Do not borrow money from the family without asking.** Always ask first.
- **Do not bring strangers into the house.** Even your friends — only with employer permission.
- **Do not work for another family in secret** ("part-time" on your off-day). This is illegal.
- **Do not borrow money from a moneylender who is not licensed** ("ah long"). This can end your work permit. If you need money, talk to us first.

## 27.8a If Someone in the House Touches You or Pressures You

**This is not on the list above. This is never your fault.**

If anyone in the house — the husband, a son, a grandfather, a visitor, anyone —

- touches your body
- asks you for sex
- shows you their body or sexual pictures
- says sexual things that frighten you
- says you will lose your job if you say no
- says he will help you if you say yes

**…that is a crime in Singapore. He is breaking the law. You are not.**

It does not matter if:
- You did not say no
- You did not fight
- You said yes because you were scared
- It happened many times
- You did not tell anyone before
- You are already pregnant

**None of these things make it your fault. None of these things mean you did something wrong.**

### What to do

1. If he is near you and you are scared right now → **call 999 (police)**. Say: "I am a domestic worker. Someone here is hurting me. Please come." You will NOT be in trouble.
2. Tell us: WhatsApp Ming Hwee **+65 8011 9456**. We will believe you. We will help you move to a safe place.
3. If you can, do not wash the clothes you were wearing. Keep them in a bag. This can help the police later.
4. Do not be alone with that person again if you can avoid it. If you have a lock on your door, use it.

**You will not lose your job for telling the truth about this. We will help you find a new family.**

## 27.8b Rules About Marriage and Pregnancy

These are **rules from the government about your work permit**. They are not about you being good or bad. We explain them so nothing surprises you.

- You need permission from MOM before you marry a Singapore citizen or PR. This is true even after your work permit finishes.
- You are only allowed to be pregnant and give birth in Singapore if you are already married to a Singapore citizen or PR, with MOM's permission.
- You will have a health check every 6 months. This check includes a pregnancy test.
- If you are pregnant and you do not have MOM's permission, your work permit will usually be cancelled and you will go home.

**If you think you might be pregnant — talk to us first.** Do not hide it and do not panic. There are different situations, and some of them have options. We will explain what is true for your case. **If you are pregnant because someone forced you or pressured you, read 27.8a above — that is a crime against you, and we will treat it that way.**

## 27.9 If You Want to Stop Working — Transfer

### You can ask to transfer to a new employer

- It is your right
- You should try to make it work first — talk to your employer, talk to Ming Hwee
- If after trying it really cannot work → ask Ming Hwee for transfer
- Transfer takes **2–4 weeks**
- **You must still be paid while you are still working.** If you are working for your employer, your salary must be paid in full — during transfer talks, right up to your last working day.
- If you stop working and stay somewhere else while waiting for a new employer, you may not earn during that waiting time. Ask us exactly what will happen in your case **before** you agree to anything, so there is no surprise.
- Any salary already owed to you from before must still be paid. Transferring does not cancel it.

### Steps:
1. WhatsApp Ming Hwee at **+65 8011 9456**
2. Tell us why
3. We will help you
4. Do NOT just leave the house — that is "absconding" and you will lose your salary, your bond, and may be sent home with bad record

## 27.10 Things to Remember

1. **You are not alone.** Ming Hwee is here for you. WhatsApp **+65 8011 9456** anytime.
2. **You have rights.** No one can take them away.
3. **You can ask for help.** Asking for help is not weak — it is smart.
4. **Tell the truth.** Even if you are scared. We will help you.
5. **Do not just leave without telling anyone** — that is "absconding" and it can cost you your salary and your permit. Talk to us first.
   **But if you are in danger, get to safety FIRST.** Leave, go to a neighbour, go to a police station, call 999. Safety comes before every other rule on this page. Tell us afterwards.
6. **Save these numbers in your phone:**
   - Police: 999
   - Ambulance: 995
   - Ming Hwee WhatsApp: +65 8011 9456
   - MOM: 1800-339-5505
