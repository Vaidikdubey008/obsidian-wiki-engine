**About This Knowledge Base**

This document is the single source of truth for how Ming Hwee delivers each of its seven core services in Singapore — the ordered steps, every document required, who signs each form, MOM touch-points, indicative timelines and fees. It is written to feed the service chartboard and to onboard sales (Family Care Consultant) and admin (Care Coordinator) staff.

Each service is presented in two layers: **a Quick Reference card** (checklist + timeline + fee at a glance) followed by **the Full Process** (step-by-step, with nationality variations for the Philippines, Indonesia and Myanmar where they differ).

**1. New Hiring**

**The full end-to-end recruitment flow.** The employer wants to bring in a helper from overseas for the first time (or has no helper right now and wants one). There is no existing helper and no existing Work Permit. Ming Hwee sources, matches, processes and lands the helper.

**Quick Reference**

| **Item**  | **Detail**                                                                                           |
|-----------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Trigger   | "I want to hire a helper", "looking for a maid", "need a helper from the Philippines"                |
| Owner     | FCC (sourcing, matching, advisory) + CC (documents, MOM, logistics) + OP (source country)            |
| Timeline  | 4–6 weeks total (incl. overseas processing)                                                          |
| MOM step  | Full Work Permit application via FDW e-Service (\$35 fee) → IPA → WP issuance                        |
| Mandatory | EOP (first-time employer) · Security bond \$5,000 · Insurance (med \$15k / PA \$60k) · Medical · SIP |

**Document checklist — at a glance**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | MING HWEE PROVIDES / PREPARES (for signature) |
|---|---|
| Employer NRIC / IC copy; Income Tax Assessment or Declaration of Monthly Income; Foreign employer: EP/S Pass + passport copy, and if newly employed a company letter (position, salary, appointment date) + tenancy agreement; Additional helper: ID copies of children / elderly (proof of care need); Candidate passport copy, medical report, school certificate (via OP) | Service Agreement; Service & Fee Schedule; Agency Fee package form; Authorisation Form (MOM work-pass transactions); Employer Particulars form; Worker's Biodata (last page); Job Offer Form (signed by maid & employer); Levy GIRO form (first-time employers only); Worker's Employment History (only if prior SG experience) |

**Full Process**

**Phase 1 — Enquiry, matching & confirmation (FCC-led)**

1.  New client enquiry received. Create employer user account and input household info and helper requirements.
2.  Employer dashboard shows closest-matched candidate profiles, package fees and contract information.
3.  Needs assessment & profiling — family composition, care requirements, living arrangements, budget, cultural preferences.
4.  FDW sourcing & AI-assisted matching — shortlist 3–5 candidates; prepare biodata packs (experience, skills, video intro, references).
5.  Video/phone interviews facilitated (with translation support); document feedback and AI-assisted interview summaries.
6.  On candidate confirmation, prompt for payment. Payment activates the hiring process and notifies all parties (candidate, OP, sales, admin).
7.  Hand over the complete package to CC for MOM submission and documentation.

**Phase 2 — MOM application & IPA (CC-led)**

|                                                                                                                                      |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **SEQUENTIAL — CRITICAL** This phase must be completed strictly in order. No step proceeds until the previous one is fully complete. |

1.  Employer digital registration — guide employer to set up Singpass and a MOM employer account.
2.  E-authorisation — employer authorises Ming Hwee (via Singpass) to apply for the helper on their behalf.
3.  EOP — if first-time hiring, employer signs up for and completes the Employers' Orientation Programme (eop.com.sg).
4.  Prepare & e-sign employer document set (see checklist above); collect candidate passport copy, medical report and school certificate from the OP.
5.  Confirm the worker is medically fit before submission.
6.  CC verifies document completeness, scans to PDF, logs in to MOM FDW e-Service, completes the 9-page form, uploads documents and pays the \$35 fee.
7.  Track the MOM dashboard daily until IPA is issued; save the IPA letter to CRM and email the IPA + contract to the OP.

**Phase 3 — Embassy & medical (OP-led, CC-tracked)**

Runs after IPA. Steps vary by source country:

| **Country** | **Embassy / contract handling after IPA**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|-------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Philippines | OP prints contract, obtains helper signature, submits to the embassy / DMW online (onlineservices.dmw.gov.ph). Scan stamped contract to CC. Book DOH-accredited clinic for fit-to-fly medical; upload cert.                                                                                                                           |
| Indonesia   | Job order via Indonesian Embassy portal (fdw.indonesianlabour.sg). On approval an EC is generated — employer signs 3 original copies of EC + 1 IPA form. Prepare embassy submission pack (3 EC copies with company chop, IPA employer & worker copy, employer IC copy, worker passport copy); runner picks up; scan approved EC back. |
| Myanmar     | Notify employer, send IPA form for signature; send IPA to Myanmar agent for worker signature and earliest departure date. Download the Security Bond Transmission form from MOM and email to the Myanmar agent (required for immigration clearance).                                                                                  |

**Phase 4 — Insurance, bond, travel & arrival (CC-led)**

1.  IPA must be signed by both employer and worker and uploaded to MOM before Work Permit issuance.
2.  Purchase FDW insurance and the \$5,000 security bond before the departure date (e.g. via Income/HLAS partner portal). Ensure the security bond is transmitted (check MOM portal).
3.  Confirm the employer's pick-up availability before asking the OP/agent to issue the air ticket.
4.  Book flights only after written confirmation from both employer and OP/candidate. Notify the transport company of the arrival date to arrange airport pick-up, medical and SIP.
5.  Prepare the handover pack: Employment Contract, Salary Schedule, Rest-Day form, Safety Agreement form, and the worker's Do's & Don'ts form.

**Phase 5 — Onboarding & follow-up**

1.  On arrival, escort the helper for SIP, medical examination and fingerprinting; register SIP within the required window.
2.  On fetch/handover day, collect the placement fee (loan) per agreed mode (PayNow / cash / cheque); complete the Orientation Checklist (e-signed by employer, maid & EA staff).
3.  Collect the Work Permit card, upload to CRM and close the case.
4.  Call both employer and helper within 1 week of arrival to check integration and satisfaction (target: 80% retention / min 1 year).

**2. Direct Hiring**

**The employer already has a specific candidate in mind** — found through a personal referral, a returning helper, or their own network — and needs professional support with Work Permit processing, MOM compliance and documentation. Ming Hwee does not source or match; it handles the entire administrative process. Fees are lower than full recruitment because the client has done the sourcing.

**Quick Reference**

| **Item**  | **Detail**                                                                                                                                                    |
|-----------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Trigger   | "I already have a helper in mind", "direct hire", "I found a maid myself"                                                                                     |
| Flow type | Ticket-capture → route to sales/admin for processing (no sourcing/matching)                                                                                   |
| Timeline  | Transfer helper already in SG: 2–3 weeks · Overseas helper: 4–6 weeks (incl. embassy + travel)                                                                |
| MOM step  | Work Permit application via MOM e-Service portal → IPA → WP issuance                                                                                          |
| Mandatory | Security bond \$5,000 (insured) · Medical insurance min \$15,000/yr · PA insurance min \$60,000/yr · Medical exam · SIP within 7-day window · Contract review |

**Document checklist — at a glance**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | MING HWEE PROVIDES / PREPARES (for signature) |
|---|---|
| Employer NRIC / IC copy; Income Tax Assessment or Declaration of Monthly Income (foreign employer: EP/S Pass + passport, or company letter + tenancy agreement); Candidate details: passport copy, medical report, school certificate; For transfer candidate already in SG: existing WP details | Service Agreement + Service & Fee Schedule (direct-hire rate); Agency Fee package form; Authorisation Form; Employer Particulars form; Worker's Biodata (last page); Job Offer Form; Levy GIRO form (first-time employers); Worker's Employment History (if prior SG experience) |

**Full Process**

1.  Capture the enquiry: confirm the employer has a specific candidate; record candidate identity and source (referral / returning / own network) and whether the candidate is overseas or already in Singapore on a valid WP.
2.  Raise a Direct Hire ticket and route to sales/admin. Set expectations: 2–3 weeks (transfer) or 4–6 weeks (overseas).
3.  E-authorisation — employer authorises Ming Hwee via Singpass to transact with MOM.
4.  Collect employer documents and prepare the e-sign document set (see checklist). Confirm candidate is medically fit.
5.  Prepare and file the Work Permit application through the MOM e-Service portal — accuracy on first submission (errors delay 2–3 weeks).
6.  Arrange the \$5,000 security bond (insured), medical insurance (min \$15,000/yr) and personal accident insurance (min \$60,000/yr) to MOM specifications; coordinate the medical examination.
7.  On IPA: overseas candidate → run the embassy step for the relevant country (as in New Hiring, Phase 3) and coordinate travel; transfer candidate → proceed to WP issuance without embassy/travel.
8.  Register the helper for the Settling-In Programme within the required 7-day window and coordinate transport to the training centre.
9.  Contract review — ensure the EC covers all MOM-required terms (salary, rest days, duties, termination). Complete handover pack and Orientation Checklist; collect WP card; close case.

|                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **KEY DIFFERENCE vs NEW HIRING** No candidate sourcing, matching or interviews. Everything else — MOM submission, bond, insurance, medical, embassy (overseas only), SIP, handover — mirrors New Hiring. |

**3. Work Permit Renewal (Work Permit Enable)**

**Renew the Work Permit of an existing helper before it expires.** MOM generates a Renewal Notification roughly 8 weeks before the WP expiry date — this notification is essential to apply. Renewal keeps a trusted helper in place and avoids processing gaps that create legal complications.

**Quick Reference**

| **Item**      | **Detail**                                                                                  |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Trigger       | "renew work permit", "WP expiring", "extend my helper's permit"                             |
| Fee           | \$695                                                                                       |
| Timeline      | 1 week quoted · actual processing ~3 days                                                   |
| Start window  | Begin 60+ days before expiry; MOM Renewal Notification issued ~8 weeks before expiry        |
| E-auth window | Authorisation link is valid 1 week only; once authorised, 2-week window to complete renewal |

**Document checklist**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | MING HWEE PROVIDES / PREPARES (for signature) |
|---|---|
| Employer's NRIC copy; Renewal Notice / Notification Letter (from MOM); Worker's new salary; Number of rest days; Helper's passport; E-authorisation via MOM site | Renewal Declaration form (to be e-signed by employer) |

**Full Process**

1.  Employer enquires to renew. Create/attach the user account and input data. MOM sends the Renewal Notification (~8 weeks before expiry) — obtain a copy.
2.  Request the required materials: Renewal Notification, employer IC copy, helper passport, new salary and number of rest days.
3.  Send the e-authorisation request; employer authorises within the 1-week link validity (else the link expires). Once authorised, there is a 2-week window to complete.
4.  Employer signs the Renewal Declaration form.
5.  Purchase insurance for the FDW (ideally before 5pm so it processes overnight); next day proceed with the renewal transaction.
6.  Once insurance is processed, input the new insurance policy number and new expiry date into the signed Renewal Declaration.
7.  Upload the signed declaration to the MOM website and submit. Temporary Work Permit and renewal confirmation are auto-generated on submission.
8.  Forward the renewal confirmation to the employer to confirm completion. (Coordinate the 6-monthly medical examination as required.)

**4. Passport Renewal (Passport Enable)**

**Renew the helper's home-country passport** while she continues working in Singapore. Handled through the relevant embassy. Process and forms differ by nationality.

**Quick Reference**

| **Nationality** | **Fee** | **Timeline**                                                                                              |
|-----------------|---------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Philippines     | \$450   | Advise clients to process ~2 months in advance; always confirm the appointment date with the runner first |
| Indonesia       | \$450   | Check available appointment dates with the runner before advising the client                              |

**Trigger:** "passport expiring", "renew my helper's passport".

**Philippines — documents**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | EMBASSY DOCS — complete & return with ORIGINAL signature |
|---|---|
| Employer's NRIC copy; Worker's WP copy; Worker's ORIGINAL passport | Contract; Undertaking Form; Annex A; Passport renewal form; OFW Info Sheet |

**Indonesia — documents**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | MING HWEE PROVIDES |
|---|---|
| Employer's NRIC copy; Worker's WP copy; Worker's passport copy | Passport form (to be completed and signed) |

**Full Process**

1.  Capture the request and confirm nationality and current passport expiry.
2.  Confirm appointment availability with the runner (PH: advise ~2 months ahead; Indo: check dates before advising).
3.  Request documents from the employer/helper per the nationality checklist above (PH requires the original passport).
4.  Prepare the embassy/agency forms and obtain the required original signatures (PH) or completed & signed form (Indo).
5.  Submit to the embassy via the runner; track the appointment and processing.
6.  On completion, return the renewed passport to the helper/employer and update records.

**5. Home Leave**

**Arrange the helper's return to her home country between contracts.** Ming Hwee manages embassy endorsement, automatic levy waiver for the leave period, deferred 6-monthly medical (if applicable) and flight coordination. Embassy endorsement is typically approved within ~10 working days.

**Quick Reference**

| **Nationality** | **Fee** | **Timeline**                                                                                             |
|-----------------|---------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Philippines     | \$400   | 4 weeks (appointment required). Urgent requests: +\$40, with proof of urgency or a letter of explanation |
| Indonesia       | \$250   | 2 weeks                                                                                                  |

**Trigger:** "home leave", "helper going home for holiday". Embassy slots fill quickly before peak travel — start early.

**Philippines — documents**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | EMBASSY DOCS — complete & return with ORIGINAL signature |
|---|---|
| Employer's NRIC copy; Worker's WP copy; Worker's ORIGINAL passport; Ticket itinerary | Contract; Undertaking Form; Annex A; Balik-Manggagawa Info Sheet; OFW Info Sheet; Home Leave OEC form |

**Indonesia — documents**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | MING HWEE PROVIDES |
|---|---|
| Employer's NRIC copy; Worker's WP copy; Worker's passport copy | Home Leave form (to be completed and signed) |

**Full Process**

1.  Capture the request via the short intake (passport & contract details). Confirm nationality and intended travel dates.
2.  Confirm embassy appointment availability with the runner; advise the client on lead time (PH 4 wk / Indo 2 wk).
3.  Request documents per the nationality checklist (PH requires original passport + ticket itinerary).
4.  Prepare embassy endorsement forms; obtain original signatures (PH) or the signed Home Leave form (Indo); submit via runner.
5.  Process the automatic levy waiver for the home-leave period and defer the 6-monthly medical if applicable.
6.  Coordinate flight booking and return travel; provide real-time updates from submission through to her return.

**6. Replacement**

**Swap an existing helper for a different one** — e.g. the current helper is not suitable and the employer wants a different candidate. The flow mirrors New Hiring for the incoming candidate, but the employer signs a Replacement form and a Replacement Services & Fees form instead of the full new-hire fee schedule.

**Quick Reference**

| **Item**          | **Detail**                                                                  |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Trigger           | "replace my helper", "current maid not suitable", "want a different helper" |
| Flow type         | Ticket-capture, then a recruitment/processing flow for the incoming helper  |
| Timeline          | ~4–6 weeks for an overseas replacement (mirrors New Hiring)                 |
| Distinctive forms | Replacement form + Replacement Services & Fees form                         |
| Mandatory         | E-authorisation · MOM submission · IPA · Insurance & bond · Medical · SIP   |

**Document checklist**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | MING HWEE PROVIDES / PREPARES (for signature) |
|---|---|
| Employer NRIC / IC copy; Income Tax Assessment or Declaration of Monthly Income (foreign employer: EP/S Pass + passport, or company letter + tenancy agreement); Additional helper: ID copies of children / elderly; Incoming candidate: passport copy, medical report, school certificate (via OP) | Replacement form (employer signs); Replacement Services & Fees form; Authorisation Form; Employer Particulars form; Worker's Biodata (last page); Job Offer Form; Worker's Employment History (if prior SG experience) |

**Full Process**

1.  Client enquires to replace the helper. Create/attach the employer account and input household info and (revised) helper requirements.
2.  Candidate evaluation — dashboard shows closest-matched profiles; initiate video interview and re-evaluate requirements.
3.  On candidate confirmation, the employer signs the Replacement form.
4.  E-authorisation — employer authorises Ming Hwee via Singpass. Fill in personal details (IC, passport, address, income, etc.).
5.  Prepare the e-sign set: Authorisation Form, Employer Particulars form, Worker's Biodata last page, Job Offer Form, Worker's Employment History (if applicable), Replacement Services & Fees form.
6.  Send the Job Offer Form to the partnering agent for the selected candidate to sign; request passport copy, medical report and school certificate. Confirm medically fit.
7.  Submit the application to MOM. On IPA, notify the employer, send the IPA/declaration form for signature; send the IPA (employee copy & declaration) to the agent for the worker to sign and provide earliest departure date. IPA must be signed and uploaded before WP issuance.
8.  Confirm employer pick-up availability; purchase insurance and bond; download the Security Bond Transmission form from MOM and send to the agent where required.
9.  Notify the transport company of the arrival date to arrange pick-up, medical and SIP; complete arrival, handover and case closure as in New Hiring.

**7. Helper Transfer**

**An existing helper, already in Singapore on a valid Work Permit, moves from her current employer to a new one without going home.** Ming Hwee assists with both outgoing transfers (releasing to a new employer) and incoming transfer hires, handling all MOM paperwork and employer matching. No embassy step and no overseas travel are involved.

|                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **CLASSIFICATION RULE** The word "transfer" together with maid/helper/FDW/employer, "change employer", or any mention of an existing helper / existing Work Permit means TRANSFER — never New Hiring. Do not ask "hiring for the first time, or have you hired one?" for a transfer. |

**Quick Reference**

| **Item**         | **Detail**                                                                                                         |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Trigger          | "transfer helper/maid", "change employer", "take over a transfer maid", "my helper wants to transfer"              |
| Flow type        | Ticket-capture → raise Transfer ticket → hand over to sales (do not quote transfer pricing or process ad hoc)      |
| Timeline         | 1–2 weeks from interview to deployment                                                                             |
| Embassy / travel | None — helper stays in Singapore                                                                                   |
| Mandatory        | New employer eligibility · E-authorisation · MOM transfer application · IPA · Bond & insurance · SIP if applicable |

**Ticket capture — fields to collect**

- Current helper's name
- Current employer status — is the current employer releasing her / has agreed?
- Work Permit (WP) number and expiry date
- Reason for transfer
- Who is enquiring — current employer / new employer / the helper herself

**Document checklist**

| REQUEST FROM EMPLOYER / CLIENT | MING HWEE PREPARES |
|---|---|
| Current helper's WP details (number & expiry); Current employer release / agreement; New employer NRIC / IC copy; New employer income proof (Income Tax Assessment / Declaration; foreign employer: EP/S Pass + passport, or company letter + tenancy agreement) | Transfer agreement between current & new employer (cost-sharing; retained 1 year and furnished to MOM on request); Authorisation Form (new employer, via Singpass); Employer Particulars form (new employer); Job Offer Form; Fresh Employment Contract, Safety Agreement, Rest-Day form for the new employer |

**Full Process**

1.  Confirm in one line that this is a transfer of an existing helper (not a new hire).
2.  Capture the ticket fields (above), conversationally, one or two at a time.
3.  Raise a Transfer ticket with all fields and hand over to sales.
4.  Matching (if the new employer needs a candidate) / confirmation (if the parties already agreed); facilitate interview where needed.
5.  New employer eligibility check and e-authorisation via Singpass.
6.  Complete the transfer agreement (cost-sharing between current and new employer) and the new employer's document set.
7.  Submit the transfer / new Work Permit application to MOM; on IPA, arrange the security bond and insurance under the new employer.
8.  Issue the new Work Permit; complete SIP if applicable; handover pack and case closure. No embassy or travel step.
