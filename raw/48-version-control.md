## 48.2 Versioning Convention

**Format:** `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`

- **MAJOR** (1.0.0 → 2.0.0): Structural change — new modules added, modules removed, major reorganisation, or fundamental policy change.
- **MINOR** (1.0.0 → 1.1.0): Content update — new scenarios added, existing scripts revised, regulatory information updated, new Q&A pairs.
- **PATCH** (1.0.0 → 1.0.1): Fix — typo correction, broken link fix, minor wording adjustment, [INSERT]/[VERIFY] item filled in.

## v1.3.1 — 31 July 2026 · Helper Routing Policy Change

**Trigger:** Ming Hwee management decision. Supersedes the dual-routing model introduced hours earlier in v1.3.0.

### Decision

**Ming Hwee is the first point of contact for helper concerns and manages every case through to resolution.** The chatbot takes the concern, reassures, captures the handoff brief, and escalates internally. It does not refer helpers to external welfare organisations.

### Changed

- **HOME, CDE and FAST removed** from all helper-facing content and from the safety allowlist — Modules 00, 01, 27, 34, 42, 44, 45, 46, 47, README, vendor brief.
- **Module 01.5 rewritten** from "dual routing" to "Ming Hwee manages", with the rationale restated around what we can actually deliver (mediation, transfer, relocation) rather than around channel choice.
- **MOM retained as a single factual line** (1800-339-5505) in the safety allowlist and in Module 27's "other numbers" table. Not promoted, not offered as an alternative. Retained for three reasons: it keeps Module 34.3's salary-recovery flow coherent (MOM conducts salary conciliation); helpers receive it at the mandatory MOM Settling-In Programme within 3 days of arrival regardless; and a MOM-licensed agency omitting the regulator entirely from a document titled *Your Rights* is an artifact that is hard to explain in a licensing review.
- **Module 45** contained an incorrect HOME number (1800-341-4357; the real one is 1800-797-7977). Removed as part of this change — but worth noting as evidence for the §5 rule in the vendor brief: an unverified contact number sat in the KB across multiple versions and four reviews.

### Strengthened — the safety carve-out

Removing alternative channels makes the emergency path the *only* route out of a dangerous situation, so it was hardened rather than left as-is:

- **999/995 first, always**, for physical assault, sexual assault or coercion, confinement, threats to life, and medical emergency. Never gated behind contacting Ming Hwee. Never deferred to office hours.
- Specified as a **deterministic Layer 1 + Layer 6 check**, explicitly not a prompt instruction. Rationale in the vendor brief §6: every other flow in the KB routes to Ming Hwee, and a model under context pressure will generalise that pattern over the exception — producing an on-brand agency-routing reply to someone describing an assault in progress.
- **New KPI (47.2b):** % of crisis conversations where 999/995 was surfaced before any agency-routing step. Target 100%. Any miss is a safety incident with a written review, not a quality nit.
- If a helper says she has already contacted MOM, the bot acknowledges neutrally and continues helping — never discourages, never suggests it affects her position.

### Standing note for future editors

The v1.3.0 audit recorded a conflict-of-interest concern: Ming Hwee is paid by employers, helpers complain about employers, and the agency is now the only channel the bot offers. Management has considered this and decided the routing above. The concern is recorded in `INDEPENDENT-AUDIT-2026-07.md` §2.1 rather than deleted, so that a future reviewer sees the reasoning on both sides and does not have to rediscover it.

**What follows from the decision:** the helper trust indicators in Module 47.2b are now the primary early-warning signal available. With no external referral path in the bot, a month-on-month fall in helper-initiated contacts is as likely to mean lost trust as improved conditions, and should be investigated rather than celebrated.

## v1.3.0 — 31 July 2026 · Regulatory Correction & Dual-Routing Release

**Trigger:** Independent audit (`INDEPENDENT-AUDIT-2026-07.md`) — the first review of this KB not conducted by its own author. It found two regulatory errors that had survived four prior self-reviews.

### 🔴 Regulatory corrections (the KB was wrong)

| # | Error | Correct position | Modules changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Non-compensable monthly rest day missing entirely | Since **1 Jan 2023**, ≥1 rest day/month cannot be compensated away. May be split into two half-days; deferrable by ≤1 month. Work Permit condition — breach is an offence. | 19, 22, 26, 27, 34, 42, 57, 58, Portal spec |
| 2 | Medical insurance minimum stated as $15,000 | **$60,000/year since 1 July 2023**, sub-limits must each meet it. **25% employer co-pay above the first $15,000.** Direct hospital billing and age-differentiated premiums since 1 July 2025. | 19, 22, 41, 43, 57, 58, Portal spec |

Portal seed data in both Module 58 and `PORTAL-API-SPECIFICATION.md` carried `medical_insurance_minimum_coverage: 15000`. Had the portal been built from that spec, it would have gone live wrong. Corrected, with a `note` field warning against re-seeding the old value.

### 🟠 Safeguarding

- **Module 27.8 rewritten.** "Do not get pregnant" and "do not have a relationship with the husband/sons" removed from the *Things You Should NEVER Do* list, where they sat between "do not hit the children" and "do not steal." That framing told a coerced helper her abuse was her misconduct.
- **New 27.8a** — explicit, simple-English "this is never your fault" section covering sexual coercion, with the non-negotiables spelled out (didn't say no, didn't fight, said yes out of fear, happened repeatedly, already pregnant — none of it changes anything).
- **New 27.8b** — marriage and pregnancy handled factually as permit conditions, with an explicit bridge back to 27.8a.
- **Module 22** pregnancy Q&A gains a handling warning and the employer's MOM notification duty.

### 🟠 Conflict of interest — dual routing

- **Module 01.5 rewritten.** Justification #5 ("Ming Hwee can mediate without the helper losing employment status, which a direct MOM complaint can risk") **deleted** — a deterrent presented as care.
- **Module 46.9 suppression clause deleted** ("mentioned only when Ming Hwee cannot serve the helper's need"). Independent channels are now always named.
- MOM / HOME / CDE added to Module 27.7, Module 34 flows, and the safety allowlist. *(HOME and CDE removed again in v1.3.1 — see above.)*
- Rationale recorded in-line so a future editor knows why, and doesn't quietly revert it.

### 🟡 Contradictions resolved

- **Passport confiscation** had three protocols (01.5 → police; 34.8 → ask employer again, then call us; 34.10 → escalate to Ming Hwee). Single canonical protocol now at **01.5a**; 22 and 34 defer to it. The "ask one more time" coaching step is removed.
- **Module 36.1 reversed** — no longer tells employers to delay a police report on a missing helper.
- **Guardrail 14** extended with an explicit SAFETY ALLOWLIST (999, 995, police non-emergency, MOM, Ming Hwee — HOME/CDE removed in v1.3.1) so helper safety numbers survive a portal outage. Everything else still portal-only.
- **Module 27.9** no longer states that reduced salary during transfer is simply to be expected.

### 🟢 Measurement

- **New Module 47.2a — Time-to-Human for a helper in distress.** Crisis <15 min 24/7; measured end-to-end; reported by hour of day; every miss reviewed in writing. Module 34 has always promised a human in 5 minutes and nothing measured it.
- **New 47.2b — Helper Trust Indicators**, including % of complaint conversations where independent channels were named.
- **Response Accuracy split** into Regulatory (100%, weekly) and General (≥95%, monthly). A 1-in-20 error rate on regulatory claims is a scheduled compliance incident, not a passing grade.
- **New Module 27.1a — placement loan.** Previously one line in Module 28 across the whole KB. Helper-facing, with the six questions she should be able to answer, her right to a copy of what she signed, and an explicit statement that owing money does not reduce her rights. Commercial terms left as `[INSERT]` — the bot routes to a human until Ming Hwee fills them in.

### Process change (the important one)

Module 57's April 2026 audit checked whether the modules agreed **with each other**. They did — five modules stated the same wrong rest-day rule, so the consistency check passed. Internal consistency cannot detect a uniform error.

**Standing rule added to Module 57:** every future audit must verify at least one regulatory fact against a primary MOM source *on the day of the audit*, recording URL and date; and at least one audit per year must be conducted by someone who did not author the KB.
