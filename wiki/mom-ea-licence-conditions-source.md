# MOM — Employment Agencies Licence Conditions (Comprehensive Licence)

## What this is
The full legal text of the licence conditions binding a Comprehensive Licence holder, plus the Select Licence conditions. The largest document in the corpus. It is the statutory backdrop to every other document here: the obligations that make wrong chatbot output a licensing risk rather than merely a customer-service one.

## Key claims
- The conditions apply to all three Comprehensive Licence categories: All, Non-Foreign Domestic Worker, and Local. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Applicability · 2026-09-01)
- The licensee must operate in accordance with the Employment Agencies Act (Cap 92), the Employment Agencies Rules 2011, the licence conditions, and any EA alerts issued by the Commissioner. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 2 · 2026-09-01)
- The licensee must at all times have at least one registered key appointment holder, and must obtain MOM's written approval before changing key appointment holders. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 3 · 2026-09-01)
- The agency's full name and licence number must appear on signboards, on every document issued including invoices, receipts, service agreements and correspondence, and in every advertisement including website and social media. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 4 · 2026-09-01)
- Client information must not be divulged without written consent, except where required for investigations under any law or by the Commissioner. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 5(a) · 2026-09-01)
- Work pass application documents must be retained for a minimum of 3 years from the date of application or renewal. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 5(c) · 2026-09-01)
- The licensee must ensure staff report, and must itself report to MOM, any known breach of the Employment Agencies Act, Employment of Foreign Manpower Act, Employment Act, or Work Injury Compensation Act committed in connection with a placement. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 6A · 2026-09-01)
- Fees or costs payable by an employer to meet MOM requirements — security bond, insurance, safety courses — must not be misrepresented, and a written breakdown of each must be provided. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 8(a) · 2026-09-01)
- Once engaged, the licensee must sign a written agreement with the employer stating accurately and clearly the breakdown of each such fee or cost. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 8(b) · 2026-09-01)
- Employer authorisation, obtained via Singpass or in prescribed written form, is required before performing any work pass transaction on the employer's behalf. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 9 · 2026-09-01)
- The foreign employee must receive her own complete copy of the In-Principle Approval letter a reasonable time before departure, the licensee must document the steps taken to ensure this, and the employee must be allowed to keep the IPA letter. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Conditions 9A–9C · 2026-09-01)
- The licensee must not enter into agreements with a foreign employee to retain or transfer her passport or work pass, except for the purpose of procuring employment. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 10 · 2026-09-01)
- The licensee bears the full cost of repatriation where a work permit is not issued or is revoked, or the foreigner is not placed, and must not require the foreigner to bear any part of that cost. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 11 · 2026-09-01)
- The licensee must not repatriate or assist repatriation without taking reasonable steps to ensure it would not frustrate a statutory claim for salary arrears or work injury compensation. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 11A · 2026-09-01)
- The licensee bears the cost of acceptable accommodation, food and medical treatment for any FDW it brings in prior to deployment, and must not pass those costs to the FDW. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 12 · 2026-09-01)
- The licensee must furnish the employer with the FDW's employment history obtained from Work Permit Online, unaltered, with written acknowledgement of receipt retained, and must not disclose it to anyone else. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 13 · 2026-09-01)
- A prescribed safety agreement between employer and FDW must be explained, agreed, signed and retained before deployment. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 13A · 2026-09-01)
- Where an employer returns an FDW intending to transfer her out, the licensee must inform the employer immediately in writing that the employer remains legally responsible, and again after 21 calendar days of providing food and accommodation. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 15 · 2026-09-01)
- From 1 July 2020, the licensee must not publicly disclose any information or photograph of an FDW on any platform, with or without her consent, except as permitted in the annexes. Full biodata and image may go directly to an employer who has specifically requested it, or via restricted-access platform. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 17 · 2026-09-01)
- From 1 December 2021, the licensee must perform post-placement checks with every matched FDW and her employer at least once within the first three months of deployment, by phone, video call or house visit. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Condition 19 · 2026-09-01)
- Records of those checks must be retained for two years and furnished to MOM on request; three failed attempts must be reported to MOM within one week, as must any issue the licensee cannot resolve. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Conditions 19B–19D · 2026-09-01)
- The Select Licence does not cover placement in jobs earning a fixed monthly salary of SGD 4,500 or less. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Select Licence, Condition 1 · 2026-09-01)

## Open questions this source raises
- Which licence category does Ming Hwee hold? Not stated in any document in this corpus, yet several obligations differ by category.
- Condition 17 forbids public disclosure of FDW information "on any platform." Does a WhatsApp conversation with a prospective employer count as restricted access, or as a platform? This directly constrains what the chatbot may send.
- Condition 19's post-placement check is a phone, video or house-visit obligation. The vendor proposal's automated day 7/30/90 surveys are none of those — do the surveys satisfy Condition 19, or is a separate human check still required?
- Annexes A through H are referenced throughout and define the actual standards. None are present in this corpus.

## Conflicts introduced
None directly. But Condition 19's human-contact requirement sits awkwardly against the proposal's automated post-placement surveys — see the open question above and [[EA-licence-compliance]].
