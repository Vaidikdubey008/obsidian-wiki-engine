# Module 48 — Version Control & Change Log

## What this is
The knowledge base's own governance module: versioning convention, release history, and the record of what each release changed. It is the document the vendor brief nominates for recording resolutions to contradictions found during the build.

## Key claims
- The KB version recorded in this module is 1.2.0, comprising 59 modules (00–58) and approximately 17,500 lines. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.1 · 2026-09-01)
- The KB was generated by Claude (Anthropic) in collaboration with Ming Hwee, initially in April 2026. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.1 · 2026-09-01)
- Both the KB Owner and the date of the first full Ming Hwee review are unfilled placeholders. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.1 · 2026-09-01)
- Versioning is MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH: MAJOR for structural or fundamental policy change, MINOR for content updates, PATCH for fixes including filling in a placeholder. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.2 · 2026-09-01)
- v1.2.0 was the Dynamic Data Architecture refactor, prompted by v1.1 carrying roughly 342 unfilled placeholders and by Ming Hwee's judgement that constant KB editing for changing government policy was "operationally fragile." (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- v1.2.0 added Module 58, defining the portal API contract and its six endpoints: placement-fees, regulatory, service-pricing, salary-ranges, partners, exchange-rates. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- v1.2.0 added Guardrail 14 to Module 01, converted Module 19 and all 30 Module 41 Q&A pairs to variable references, and rewrote Module 46 from an embedded data store into a directory of authoritative sources. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- v1.2.0 added "Failure 0: Portal API down" to Module 55, requiring the bot to route to a human rather than quote stale numbers. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- Runtime variables now resolved from the portal include the standard and concessionary monthly levy, elderly and child concession thresholds, security bond, medical and personal-accident insurance minimums, work permit application and issuance fees, SIP and EOP cost ranges, salary ranges, air ticket estimates, placement fees, embassy contacts, and partner lists. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- Operational impact: a MOM levy change requires one portal update and reaches the chatbot within an hour, with no KB redeploy. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- Human action still required: quarterly review of portal data against the MOM website (30–45 minutes), annual review of embassy and partner data, and monitoring of the chatbot fallback rate. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- v1.2.0 cannot be deployed until seven portal prerequisites exist, including the five database tables, the six REST endpoints, the admin panel, bearer-token authentication, initial data population, a designated quarterly sync owner, and API health monitoring. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- The reviewer and approver fields for v1.2.0 are unfilled placeholders. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
- v1.1.0 addressed 7 structural gaps and 4 content weaknesses identified after v1.0.0's 49 modules, adding 9 new modules across 4 sprints. (src: raw/48-version-control.md §48.3 · 2026-09-01)
