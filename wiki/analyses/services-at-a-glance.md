# Services at a glance — routing the seven services

## Question
Given an enquiry, which of Ming Hwee's seven services does it belong to, and what should the client be told about time and cost?

## Short answer
Route on three facts: is there an existing helper / existing WP, is the helper already in Singapore, and did the client bring their own candidate. Those three answers separate New Hiring, Direct Hiring, Transfer, Renewal, Passport Renewal, Home Leave and Replacement. When "transfer" or an existing WP is mentioned, it is always [[Helper-Transfer]], never [[New-Hiring]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §Services at a Glance; §7 · 2026-09-08)

## Comparison table
| Service | When it applies | Helper in SG? | Embassy? | Indicative time | Fee (from) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[New-Hiring]] | First-time overseas hire; no existing helper | No | Yes | 4–6 weeks | Package |
| [[Direct-Hiring]] | Employer already has a specific candidate | Transfer: yes / Overseas: no | Overseas only | 2–3 wk (transfer) · 4–6 wk (overseas) | Lower than full |
| [[Work-Permit-Renewal]] | Existing helper, WP expiring | Yes | No | 1 week (actual ~3 days) | $695 |
| [[Passport-Renewal]] | Helper's passport expiring | Yes | Yes (PH) | PH ~2 months ahead · Indo check runner | PH $450 · Indo $450 |
| [[Home-Leave]] | Helper returning home between contracts | Yes | Yes (PH) | PH 4 wk · Indo 2 wk | PH $400 · Indo $250 |
| [[Replacement]] | Swap current helper for a different one | New one: no (overseas) | Yes | ~4–6 weeks | Replacement fee |
| [[Helper-Transfer]] | Existing helper moves to a new employer, stays in SG | Yes | No | 1–2 weeks | Transfer fee |

(src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §Services at a Glance · 2026-09-08)

## Reasoning
Only New Hiring is a full "deep flow"; the other six are ticket-capture flows that gather details and route to sales/admin, reusing shared building blocks. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §About · 2026-09-08) Those building blocks are consistent across services: e-authorisation (Singpass), MOM FDW e-Service ($35), IPA, a $5,000 insured security bond, medical insurance min $15,000/yr, PA insurance min $60,000/yr, a medical exam, SIP for the helper, and EOP for first-time employers. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §Services at a Glance · 2026-09-08) The embassy step is what separates overseas-candidate flows (New Hiring, overseas Direct Hiring, Replacement, and PH passport/home-leave) from in-SG flows (Renewal, Transfer). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §Services at a Glance · 2026-09-08)

## Trade-offs / routing traps
| Situation | Correct route | Trap to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| "I want to transfer my maid" / existing WP mentioned | [[Helper-Transfer]] | Never treat as New Hiring; skip the first-time-employer question |
| Client already has a candidate, in SG on a valid WP | [[Direct-Hiring]] (or Transfer) | Not full New Hiring — no sourcing/matching |
| WP expiring soon | [[Work-Permit-Renewal]] | Tight 1-week auth link + 2-week completion window |
| Current helper unsuitable, wants another | [[Replacement]] | Use Replacement forms, not the new-hire fee schedule |

(src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2, §3, §6, §7 · 2026-09-08)

## Confidence and gaps
The routing logic is directly stated. What is not settled: the actual fee figures for New Hiring, Direct Hiring, Replacement and Transfer (all "package"/"fee"), and whether every stated figure still matches the live MOM portal — the source itself warns these change. Per AGENTS.md §0.2 all figures here require verification before quoting. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §About NOTE · 2026-09-08)
