# MOM — Guidelines for Employment Agencies Placing FDWs

## What this is
MOM's official conduct guidelines for employment agencies that place foreign domestic workers. A primary regulatory source, not a Ming Hwee document. It sets the professional standards that Ming Hwee — and by extension any chatbot speaking on Ming Hwee's behalf — must not breach.

## Key claims
- A breach of any provision of these Guidelines constitutes an action detrimental to the interest of the EA's client, and may result in demerit points and the suspension or revocation of the EA's licence. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Introduction · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must act with honesty, fidelity and integrity, act on the client's instructions, and honour its agreements. Both the employer and the FDW are "clients." (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients · 2026-09-01)
- Where an FDW leaves her employer's home without informing the employer, the EA must inform the employer of her whereabouts immediately and inform MOM by the next working day. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients · 2026-09-01)
- Prompt assistance is mandatory: non-payment of salary is referred to MOM and allegations of abuse are referred to the Singapore Police Force, immediately or by the next working day. Employment relationship issues are mediated within 7 calendar days of notifying MOM. Accommodation must be provided if needed. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must educate both employer and FDW before deployment that the FDW keeps her own Work Permit card, that the employer must not keep her salary or money on her behalf, that her mobile phone is her personal property, and that her cultural and religious practices should be respected. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must not mislead clients or provide inaccurate information, and the guidelines name giving "wrong advice on MOM's regulations to employers and FDWs" as the example. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must not declare inaccurate contact details in work permit applications, including substituting EA personnel contacts for the employer's own. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must not withhold relevant information affecting hiring decisions — the worked example is an elderly family member requiring round-the-clock care not being disclosed to the FDW. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must not use threatening or harassing tactics, with the named example being threatening to prevent an FDW from transferring employers. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients · 2026-09-01)
- Documents must be explained, filled in fully before signature is sought, given to the client in copy, and conveyed in a language the client understands. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Signing of documents · 2026-09-01)
- Advertising must be legal, decent, honest and truthful. FDW biodata may be shown only on request, requires written consent before being posted online, and must not be viewable by passers-by. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Advertising Practices · 2026-09-01)
- Prohibited advertising content includes mention of fees, salaries or loan amounts; merchandise-like terms such as "free replacement" and "fast delivery"; and subjective traits such as "obedient", "compliant", "smart" or "hard working". (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Advertising Practices · 2026-09-01)
- Acceptable advertising claims include placement volume, transfer or retention rates, types of training provided, and verifiable facts. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Advertising Practices · 2026-09-01)
- Ongoing duties to the FDW include conveying job demands accurately, obtaining consent before submitting work pass applications, advising her on responsible ways to seek help including in cases of physical abuse, providing EA contact details, remaining contactable, and rendering support for at least the duration of the work permit (normally two years). (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Ongoing Duties to FDW · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must not facilitate downward revision of salary or rest days without the FDW's consent, and must inform MOM where such a revision occurred. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Ongoing Duties to FDW · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must not advise or encourage employers to withhold rest days. Where a genuine need requires work on a rest day, the arrangement must be known upfront and agreed by the FDW. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Ongoing Duties to employer · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must not advise employers to keep the FDW's passport. MOM records instances of employers being wrongly advised to do so by their EAs. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Ongoing Duties to employer · 2026-09-01)
- The EA must give employers comprehensive information on FDW insurance including coverage and exclusions, and refund unused premiums on premature termination. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §Ongoing Duties to employer · 2026-09-01)

## Open questions this source raises
- The guidelines require the EA to remain contactable and render help promptly. Does a chatbot count as "contactable" for this purpose outside office hours, or does the obligation require a human?
- The runaway-helper duty (inform employer immediately, MOM next working day) is not covered by any chatbot flow in the corpus. Which system owns it?
- Guardrail 6 forbids the chatbot naming Ming Hwee staff, while the licence conditions require agency personnel names and registration numbers on documents. Do these overlap anywhere the bot generates output?

## Conflicts introduced
None. This is a primary regulatory source and takes precedence over agency documents where they differ.
