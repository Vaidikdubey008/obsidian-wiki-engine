# Replacement

## Definition
Swap an existing helper for a different one — for example, the current helper is not suitable and the employer wants a different candidate. The flow mirrors [[New-Hiring]] for the incoming candidate, but the employer signs a Replacement form and a Replacement Services & Fees form instead of the full new-hire fee schedule. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §6. Replacement · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
It is New Hiring's recruitment/processing flow re-run for the incoming helper, distinguished only by its fee paperwork. The substance to get right is the same MOM/bond/insurance/embassy chain; the trap is billing it as a fresh new hire rather than a replacement.

## Full process
(src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §6. Replacement · 2026-09-08)

1. Client enquires to replace the helper; create/attach the employer account and input household info and revised requirements.
2. Candidate evaluation — dashboard shows closest-matched profiles; initiate a video interview and re-evaluate requirements.
3. On candidate confirmation, the employer signs the Replacement form.
4. E-authorisation via Singpass; fill in personal details (IC, passport, address, income, etc.).
5. Prepare the e-sign set: Authorisation Form, Employer Particulars form, Worker's Biodata (last page), Job Offer Form, Worker's Employment History (if applicable), Replacement Services & Fees form.
6. Send the Job Offer Form to the partnering agent for the selected candidate to sign; request passport copy, medical report and school certificate; confirm medically fit.
7. Submit to MOM. On IPA, notify the employer and send the IPA/declaration form for signature; send the IPA (employee copy & declaration) to the agent for the worker to sign and give the earliest departure date. IPA must be signed and uploaded before WP issuance.
8. Confirm employer pick-up availability; purchase insurance and bond; download the Security Bond Transmission form from MOM and send to the agent where required.
9. Notify the transport company of the arrival date to arrange pick-up, medical and SIP; complete arrival, handover and case closure as in [[New-Hiring]].

## Documents
Employer-side: NRIC/IC copy, Income Tax Assessment or Declaration of Monthly Income (foreign employer: EP/S Pass + passport, or company letter + tenancy agreement), care-need ID copies for an additional helper, and the incoming candidate's passport/medical/school certificate via the OP. Prepared: Replacement form, Replacement Services & Fees form, Authorisation Form, Employer Particulars form, Worker's Biodata (last page), Job Offer Form, Worker's Employment History (if prior SG experience). See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §6. Replacement · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- The only real difference from [[New-Hiring]] is the two Replacement forms in place of the new-hire fee schedule — everything downstream is identical, so don't re-derive the MOM chain, reuse it.
- Same volatile figures as New Hiring ($5,000 bond, $15,000/$60,000 insurance minimums) — verify against [[Ministry-of-Manpower]] before quoting.
