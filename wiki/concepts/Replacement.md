# Replacement

## Definition
Swap an existing helper for a different one — e.g. the current helper is not suitable and the employer wants a different candidate. The flow mirrors [[New-Hiring]] for the incoming candidate, but the employer signs a Replacement form and a Replacement Services & Fees form instead of the full new-hire fee schedule. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
Replacement is a recruitment/processing flow wearing different paperwork: the same MOM submission, IPA, insurance/bond, medical and SIP machinery as New Hiring, distinguished by two forms. The distinctive fields are the Replacement form and the Replacement Services & Fees form. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)

## Quick reference
- Trigger: "replace my helper", "current maid not suitable", "want a different helper". (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
- Flow type: ticket-capture, then a recruitment/processing flow for the incoming helper. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
- Timeline: ~4–6 weeks for an overseas replacement (mirrors New Hiring). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
- Distinctive forms: Replacement form + Replacement Services & Fees form. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
- Mandatory: e-authorisation, MOM submission, IPA, insurance & bond, medical, SIP. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)

## Process
1. Client enquires to replace; create/attach the employer account and input household info and revised helper requirements. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
2. Candidate evaluation — dashboard shows closest-matched profiles; initiate a video interview and re-evaluate requirements. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
3. On confirmation, the employer signs the Replacement form. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
4. E-authorisation via Singpass; fill in personal details (IC, passport, address, income, etc.). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
5. Prepare the e-sign set: Authorisation Form, Employer Particulars, Worker's Biodata last page, Job Offer Form, Employment History (if applicable), Replacement Services & Fees form. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
6. Send the Job Offer Form to the partnering agent for the candidate to sign; request passport, medical report and school cert; confirm medically fit. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
7. Submit to MOM. On IPA, notify the employer and send the IPA/declaration for signature; send the IPA (employee copy & declaration) to the agent for worker signature and earliest departure date. IPA must be signed and uploaded before WP issuance. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
8. Confirm pick-up availability; purchase insurance and bond; download the Security Bond Transmission form from MOM and send to the agent where required. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
9. Notify transport of the arrival date for pick-up, medical and SIP; complete arrival, handover and closure as in New Hiring. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)

## Documents
Employer supplies IC, income proof (or EP/S Pass + company letter + tenancy), care-need IDs for an additional helper, and — via the OP — incoming candidate passport, medical report and school cert. Ming Hwee prepares the Replacement form, Replacement Services & Fees form, Authorisation Form, Employer Particulars, Worker's Biodata, Job Offer Form and Employment History (if applicable). See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- The only real divergence from New Hiring is the two Replacement forms — don't bill the full new-hire fee schedule. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 · 2026-09-08)
- Security Bond Transmission form is downloaded from MOM and sent to the agent for overseas incoming candidates (as with Myanmar/Indonesia in New Hiring). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §6 step 8 · 2026-09-08)
