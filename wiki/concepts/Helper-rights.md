# Helper Rights

## Definition
The entitlements a migrant domestic worker holds in Singapore, written for the helper herself at Grade 6 reading level so she can read them directly. "You should know these. They protect you." (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §Overview · 2026-09-01)

## Why it matters here
This is the content the chatbot serves to its highest-stakes and least-protected user. Getting it wrong is not a service failure — the vendor brief designates the helper in distress as the user to design and test for first.

## The entitlements
(src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.1–27.6 · 2026-09-01)

| Right | What she gets | What is not OK |
|---|---|---|
| Salary | Contract amount, monthly, in full, by the contract date or within 7 days | Repeated late payment, underpayment, deductions for breakages or food not in the contract |
| Rest day | See [[Rest-day-entitlement]] — two distinct rules | Cancelling off-days; paying in lieu so she never gets a real one |
| Passport | It belongs to her; she keeps it | Employer holds it, or "I'll return it when the contract ends" — see [[Passport-confiscation-protocol]] |
| Food | 3 meals a day, enough, and food she can eat within her dietary and religious needs | Hungry daily, leftovers only, or losing weight |
| Room and sleep | A mattress, pillow, blanket, storage, privacy when changing, at least 7 hours' sleep | Sleeping on the floor, in the kitchen or bathroom, or in a room with a male family member |
| Phone | Her own phone; she can call her family | Employer takes it, bans it entirely, or reads her messages |

Reasonable employer limits are explicitly acknowledged as fair — no phone during work, quiet hours when a baby is sleeping, an agreed return time on her day off. The module distinguishes house rules from control.

## Placement loan
Treated separately because it is the largest financial question of her first year and every figure is missing. See [[Placement-loan]].

## Safety routing
Emergencies go to 999/995. Everything else goes to Ming Hwee — see [[Crisis-escalation]] and [[Agency-first-support]]. The module adds: "We know your case. We can talk to your employer. We can move you if we have to. This is what we do — you are not troubling us, and you are not in any trouble for asking." (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.7 · 2026-09-01)

## Sexual harassment and assault
Handled with unusual directness. Sexual contact or pressure by anyone in the household is a crime, and it remains so regardless of whether she said no, fought back, consented out of fear, has not reported it before, or is already pregnant. "None of these things make it your fault." She will not lose her job for reporting it. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.8a · 2026-09-01)

Practical guidance included: call 999 with a specific script, contact Ming Hwee, preserve clothing unwashed in a bag, and avoid being alone with that person. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.8a · 2026-09-01)

## Rules that constrain her
The module also states, without moralising, the conduct that can end a placement or lead to prosecution: harming children, theft, lying about serious matters, borrowing without asking, bringing strangers into the house, unauthorised part-time work, and borrowing from unlicensed moneylenders. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.8 · 2026-09-01)

Marriage and pregnancy rules are framed as work-permit conditions rather than judgements: MOM permission is required to marry a Singapore citizen or PR, a six-monthly health check includes a pregnancy test, and an unpermitted pregnancy usually results in permit cancellation. "If you think you might be pregnant — talk to us first." (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.8b · 2026-09-01)

Acceptance testing requires pregnancy disclosure to **never** be treated as misconduct, and to check for coercion. (src: raw/START-HERE-VENDOR-BRIEF.md §8 · 2026-09-01)

## Agency duties that back these rights
(src: raw/20250819 - MOM guidelines-for-eas-placing-fdws (1).docx §General duty to clients, §Ongoing Duties to FDW · 2026-09-01)

- Educate both parties before deployment that she keeps her Work Permit card, that the employer must not hold her salary, that her phone is her property, and that her cultural and religious practices are respected
- Convey job demands accurately, especially challenging caregiving duties
- Advise her on responsible ways to seek help, including in cases of physical abuse
- Remain contactable and render help promptly for at least the work permit duration (normally two years)
- Not facilitate downward revision of salary or rest days without her consent

She must also receive her own complete copy of the In-Principle Approval letter before departure and be allowed to keep it. (src: raw/20250819 - MOM-EA-licence-conditions (1).docx §Conditions 9A–9C · 2026-09-01)

Medical leave must be respected: no work during certified sick leave, salary continues. (src: raw/comprehensive-hr-agency-knowledge-base (1).docx §7.1 · 2026-09-01)

## Gotchas
- Helper-facing output must hold Grade 8 or below, and Grade 6 where limited English is detected. This is an acceptance criterion, not a style preference. (src: raw/START-HERE-VENDOR-BRIEF.md §8 · 2026-09-01)
- Burmese is unsupported, so a Myanmar helper receives this content in simple English — the cohort with the least English and the highest isolation risk. See [[Multi-language-support]]. (src: raw/START-HERE-VENDOR-BRIEF.md §9 · 2026-09-01)
- The module tells her Ming Hwee "can move you if we have to", but no transfer process or timeframe appears anywhere in this corpus. That is a promise the bot will repeat without being able to qualify it.
