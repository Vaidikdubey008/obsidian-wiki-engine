# Direct Hiring

## Definition
The employer already has a specific candidate in mind — through a personal referral, a returning helper, or their own network — and needs professional support with Work Permit processing, MOM compliance and documentation. Ming Hwee does not source or match; it handles the entire administrative process. Fees are lower than full recruitment because the client did the sourcing. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §2. Direct Hiring · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
It is the administrative half of [[New-Hiring]] with the recruitment half removed, so it is the cleanest illustration of the shared building blocks. It also forks on one question — is the candidate overseas or already in Singapore — which decides whether the embassy and travel steps run at all.

## Flow type and timeline
Ticket-capture: route to sales/admin, no sourcing/matching. Transfer helper already in SG: 2–3 weeks. Overseas helper: 4–6 weeks including embassy and travel. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §2. Direct Hiring · 2026-09-08)

## Full process
(src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §2. Direct Hiring · 2026-09-08)

1. Capture the enquiry: confirm the employer has a specific candidate; record candidate identity and source (referral / returning / own network) and whether the candidate is overseas or already in SG on a valid WP.
2. Raise a Direct Hire ticket, route to sales/admin, and set the 2–3 wk / 4–6 wk expectation.
3. E-authorisation via Singpass.
4. Collect employer documents, prepare the e-sign set, confirm the candidate is medically fit.
5. File the WP application through the MOM e-Service portal — accuracy on first submission matters (each error delays 2–3 weeks).
6. Arrange the $5,000 security bond (insured), medical insurance (min $15,000/yr) and PA insurance (min $60,000/yr) to MOM specs; coordinate the medical exam.
7. On IPA: overseas candidate → run the [[New-Hiring]] Phase 3 embassy step for the relevant country and coordinate travel; transfer candidate → proceed straight to WP issuance, no embassy/travel.
8. Register for SIP within the required 7-day window and coordinate transport to the training centre.
9. Contract review — ensure the EC covers all MOM-required terms (salary, rest days, duties, termination); complete the handover pack and Orientation Checklist; collect the WP card; close the case.

**Key difference vs New Hiring:** no candidate sourcing, matching or interviews; everything else mirrors [[New-Hiring]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §2. Direct Hiring · 2026-09-08)

## Documents
Employer-side: NRIC/IC copy, Income Tax Assessment or Declaration of Monthly Income (foreign employer: EP/S Pass + passport, or company letter + tenancy agreement), candidate passport/medical/school certificate, and for a transfer candidate already in SG the existing WP details. Prepared for signature: Service Agreement + Service & Fee Schedule (direct-hire rate), Agency Fee package form, Authorisation Form, Employer Particulars form, Worker's Biodata (last page), Job Offer Form, Levy GIRO form (first-time employers), Worker's Employment History (if prior SG experience). See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §2. Direct Hiring · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- A "transfer candidate already in SG" inside Direct Hiring is distinct from the standalone [[Helper-Transfer]] service; here the employer names the candidate, whereas Transfer is about moving an existing helper between employers. Watch the classification.
- The $15,000/yr insurance minimum conflicts with the SGD 60,000 minimum on [[Ministry-of-Manpower]] — verify before quoting.
- SIP is stated here as a 7-day window; other corpus material says 3 working days. Confirm the MOM rule.
