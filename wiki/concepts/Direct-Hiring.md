# Direct Hiring

## Definition
The employer already has a specific candidate in mind — from a referral, a returning helper, or their own network — and needs administrative support only: Work Permit processing, MOM compliance and documentation. Ming Hwee does not source or match; fees are lower than full recruitment because the client did the sourcing. It runs as a ticket-capture flow. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
Direct Hiring is New Hiring minus the sourcing/matching/interview front end. Everything downstream — MOM submission, bond, insurance, medical, embassy (overseas only), SIP, handover — mirrors [[New-Hiring]]. Routing an enquiry here instead of New Hiring turns on one fact: does the employer already have a candidate? (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 KEY DIFFERENCE · 2026-09-08)

## Quick reference
- Trigger: "I already have a helper in mind", "direct hire", "I found a maid myself". (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
- Flow type: ticket-capture → route to sales/admin (no sourcing/matching). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
- Timeline: transfer candidate already in SG 2–3 weeks; overseas candidate 4–6 weeks incl. embassy + travel. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
- MOM step: WP application via MOM e-Service portal → IPA → WP issuance. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
- Mandatory: $5,000 bond (insured), medical insurance min $15k/yr, PA insurance min $60k/yr, medical exam, SIP within a 7-day window, contract review. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)

## Process
1. Capture the enquiry: confirm a specific candidate; record identity and source; note whether the candidate is overseas or already in SG on a valid WP. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
2. Raise a Direct Hire ticket; set expectations (2–3 wk transfer / 4–6 wk overseas). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
3. E-authorisation via Singpass. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
4. Collect employer documents and prepare the e-sign set; confirm candidate medically fit. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
5. File the WP application through the MOM e-Service portal — accuracy on first submission matters (errors delay 2–3 weeks). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
6. Arrange the $5,000 bond, medical insurance and PA insurance to MOM specs; coordinate the medical. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
7. On IPA: overseas candidate → run the embassy step (as in [[New-Hiring]] Phase 3) and coordinate travel; transfer candidate → proceed to WP issuance with no embassy/travel. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
8. Register SIP within the 7-day window; coordinate transport to the training centre. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)
9. Contract review (salary, rest days, duties, termination); complete handover pack and Orientation Checklist; collect WP card; close case. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)

## Documents
Employer supplies IC, income proof (or EP/S Pass + company letter + tenancy for foreign employers), candidate passport/medical/school cert, and existing WP details for an in-SG transfer candidate. Ming Hwee prepares the Service Agreement + Service & Fee Schedule (direct-hire rate), Agency Fee form, Authorisation Form, Employer Particulars, Worker's Biodata, Job Offer Form, Levy GIRO (first-timers) and Employment History (if applicable). See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- First-submission accuracy: MOM errors cost 2–3 weeks. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §2 step 5 · 2026-09-08)
- A candidate already in SG on a valid WP is effectively a transfer — see [[Helper-Transfer]] for the dedicated flow and its classification rule. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
