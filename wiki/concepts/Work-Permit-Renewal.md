# Work Permit Renewal

## Definition
Renew the Work Permit of an existing helper before it expires. MOM generates a Renewal Notification roughly 8 weeks before the WP expiry date, and this notification is essential to apply. Renewal keeps a trusted helper in place and avoids processing gaps that create legal complications. Also called "Work Permit Enable". (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
This is the most time-boxed of the seven services: two short windows (a 1-week authorisation link and a 2-week completion window) sit on top of the ~8-week MOM notification lead time. Missing them forces a restart or risks a permit lapse. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)

## Quick reference
- Trigger: "renew work permit", "WP expiring", "extend my helper's permit". (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
- Fee: $695. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
- Timeline: 1 week quoted; actual processing ~3 days. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
- Start window: begin 60+ days before expiry; MOM Renewal Notification issued ~8 weeks before expiry. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
- E-auth window: authorisation link valid 1 week only; once authorised, a 2-week window to complete. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)

## Process
1. Employer enquires; create/attach the account. Obtain a copy of the MOM Renewal Notification (~8 weeks before expiry). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
2. Request materials: Renewal Notification, employer IC copy, helper passport, new salary, number of rest days. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
3. Send the e-authorisation request; employer authorises within the 1-week validity, then a 2-week window to complete. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
4. Employer signs the Renewal Declaration form. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
5. Purchase FDW insurance (ideally before 5pm so it processes overnight); proceed with the renewal transaction the next day. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
6. Once insurance is processed, enter the new policy number and expiry date into the signed Renewal Declaration. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
7. Upload the signed declaration to the MOM website and submit; a Temporary Work Permit and renewal confirmation are auto-generated on submission. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)
8. Forward the renewal confirmation to the employer; coordinate the 6-monthly medical as required. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)

## Documents
Employer supplies IC copy, MOM Renewal Notice, worker's new salary, number of rest days, helper's passport, and e-authorisation via the MOM site. Ming Hwee prepares the Renewal Declaration form for e-signature. See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- Insurance timing: buy before 5pm so it clears overnight, or the transaction slips a day. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 step 5 · 2026-09-08)
- The 1-week authorisation link is the tightest constraint — if it expires the employer must re-authorise. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §3 step 3 · 2026-09-08)
- No embassy step: the helper is already in Singapore. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §Services at a Glance · 2026-09-08)
- The $695 fee is a stated figure subject to change — re-confirm before quoting. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §About NOTE · 2026-09-08)
