# Home Leave

## Definition
Arrange the helper's return to her home country between contracts. Ming Hwee manages embassy endorsement, the automatic levy waiver for the leave period, a deferred 6-monthly medical (if applicable) and flight coordination. Embassy endorsement is typically approved within ~10 working days. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
Home Leave pairs an embassy endorsement with a MOM-side levy waiver, so it touches both the runner workflow and [[Ministry-of-Manpower]] levy handling. Embassy slots fill quickly before peak travel, so lead time is the main risk. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)

## Process
1. Capture via short intake (passport & contract details); confirm nationality and travel dates. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)
2. Confirm embassy appointment availability with the runner; advise lead time (PH 4 wk / Indo 2 wk). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)
3. Request documents per nationality (PH requires original passport + ticket itinerary). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)
4. Prepare embassy endorsement forms; obtain original signatures (PH) or the signed Home Leave form (Indo); submit via runner. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)
5. Process the automatic levy waiver for the leave period; defer the 6-monthly medical if applicable. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)
6. Coordinate flight booking and return travel; give real-time updates through to her return. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)

## Documents
- **Philippines** — employer NRIC copy, worker's WP copy, worker's ORIGINAL passport, ticket itinerary; embassy docs (Contract, Undertaking Form, Annex A, Balik-Manggagawa Info Sheet, OFW Info Sheet, Home Leave OEC form) with original signature. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)
- **Indonesia** — employer NRIC copy, worker's WP copy, worker's passport copy; Ming Hwee provides the Home Leave form to complete and sign. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)

See [[Forms-and-documents-register]].

## Gotchas
- Start early — embassy slots vanish before peak travel. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)
- PH urgent surcharge (+$40) needs proof of urgency or a letter of explanation. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 · 2026-09-08)
- The levy waiver is automatic for the leave period but must be actioned; don't forget the deferred 6-monthly medical. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §5 step 5 · 2026-09-08)
