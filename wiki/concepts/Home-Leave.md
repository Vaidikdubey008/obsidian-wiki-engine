# Home Leave

## Definition
Arrange the helper's return to her home country between contracts. Ming Hwee manages embassy endorsement, the automatic levy waiver for the leave period, the deferred 6-monthly medical (if applicable) and flight coordination. Embassy endorsement is typically approved within ~10 working days. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §5. Home Leave · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
It carries a regulatory benefit most services don't — an automatic levy waiver for the leave period — so mishandling it costs the employer money directly. Like [[Passport-Renewal]], it is embassy- and runner-gated, and the source warns that slots fill quickly before peak travel.

## Full process
(src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §5. Home Leave · 2026-09-08)

1. Capture the request via the short intake (passport & contract details); confirm nationality and intended travel dates.
2. Confirm embassy appointment availability with the runner; advise lead time (PH 4 wk / Indo 2 wk).
3. Request documents per the nationality checklist (PH requires the ORIGINAL passport + ticket itinerary).
4. Prepare embassy endorsement forms; obtain original signatures (PH) or the signed Home Leave form (Indo); submit via runner.
5. Process the automatic levy waiver for the home-leave period and defer the 6-monthly medical if applicable.
6. Coordinate flight booking and return travel; provide real-time updates from submission through to her return.

## Documents
- **Philippines** — requested: employer's NRIC copy, worker's WP copy, worker's ORIGINAL passport, ticket itinerary. Embassy docs with original signature: Contract, Undertaking Form, Annex A, Balik-Manggagawa Info Sheet, OFW Info Sheet, Home Leave OEC form.
- **Indonesia** — requested: employer's NRIC copy, worker's WP copy, worker's passport copy. Ming Hwee provides: Home Leave form (completed and signed).

See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §5. Home Leave · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- The levy waiver is automatic for the leave period — don't forget to process it; it is the employer's saving and the reason to log the exact leave dates.
- PH requires the ORIGINAL passport and the ticket itinerary; the itinerary is a prerequisite, so booking and endorsement interleave.
- Only PH and Indonesia are documented; Myanmar home leave is not covered here.
