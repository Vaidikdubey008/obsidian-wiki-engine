# New Hiring

## Definition
The full end-to-end recruitment flow: a Singapore employer brings in a helper from overseas for the first time (or has no helper and wants one), with no existing helper and no existing Work Permit. Ming Hwee sources, matches, processes and lands the helper. It is the only one of the seven services run as a "deep flow" rather than ticket-capture. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
New Hiring is the reference process the other services borrow from — its Phase 2 (MOM/IPA), Phase 3 (embassy) and Phase 4 (insurance/bond/arrival) blocks recur in [[Direct-Hiring]] and [[Replacement]]. Getting this flow right defines the shared building blocks used everywhere else. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §About · 2026-09-08)

## Quick reference
- Trigger: "I want to hire a helper", "looking for a maid", "need a helper from the Philippines". (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 · 2026-09-08)
- Owner: FCC (sourcing, matching, advisory) + CC (documents, MOM, logistics) + OP (source country). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 · 2026-09-08)
- Timeline: 4–6 weeks total incl. overseas processing. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 · 2026-09-08)
- MOM step: full Work Permit application via FDW e-Service ($35) → IPA → WP issuance. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 · 2026-09-08)
- Mandatory: EOP (first-time employer), $5,000 security bond, insurance (med $15k / PA $60k), medical, SIP. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 · 2026-09-08)

## Process (five phases)
- **Phase 1 — Enquiry, matching & confirmation (FCC-led):** create employer account and capture household/helper requirements; needs assessment and profiling; AI-assisted matching shortlists 3–5 candidates with biodata packs; facilitate video/phone interviews with translation; on confirmation take payment (which activates the process and notifies candidate, OP, sales and admin); hand the package to CC. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 1 · 2026-09-08)
- **Phase 2 — MOM application & IPA (CC-led), strictly sequential:** employer Singpass + MOM account → e-authorisation → EOP if first-time → prepare and e-sign the employer document set and collect candidate passport/medical/school cert from the OP → confirm medically fit → CC completes the 9-page FDW e-Service form, uploads documents and pays $35 → track MOM dashboard daily until IPA, save to CRM and email IPA + contract to the OP. No step proceeds until the previous one is complete. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 2 · 2026-09-08)
- **Phase 3 — Embassy & medical (OP-led, CC-tracked):** runs after IPA and varies by source country (see [[Passport-Renewal]] and [[Home-Leave]] for the same embassy machinery reused). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 3 · 2026-09-08)
- **Phase 4 — Insurance, bond, travel & arrival (CC-led):** IPA signed by employer and worker and uploaded before WP issuance; purchase FDW insurance and the $5,000 bond before departure and confirm the bond is transmitted; confirm employer pick-up availability before the OP issues the air ticket; book flights only on written confirmation from both sides; prepare the handover pack (EC, Salary Schedule, Rest-Day form, Safety Agreement, Do's & Don'ts). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 4 · 2026-09-08)
- **Phase 5 — Onboarding & follow-up:** escort helper for SIP, medical and fingerprinting; collect placement fee (loan) at handover; complete the Orientation Checklist; collect the WP card and close the case; call employer and helper within 1 week (target 80% retention / min 1 year). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 5 · 2026-09-08)

## Embassy step by source country (Phase 3)
| Country | Handling after IPA | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Philippines | OP prints contract, gets helper signature, submits to embassy / DMW online; scan stamped contract to CC; DOH-accredited fit-to-fly medical, upload cert. | (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 3 · 2026-09-08) |
| Indonesia | Job order via Indonesian Embassy portal; on approval an EC is generated, employer signs 3 original EC copies + 1 IPA form; embassy pack (3 EC with company chop, IPA copies, employer IC, worker passport) picked up by runner; scan approved EC back. | (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 3 · 2026-09-08) |
| Myanmar | Send IPA form for employer signature; send IPA to Myanmar agent for worker signature and earliest departure date; download the Security Bond Transmission form from MOM and email it to the Myanmar agent (required for immigration clearance). | (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 3 · 2026-09-08) |

## Documents
The employer supplies IC, income proof (or EP/S Pass + company letter + tenancy for foreign employers), care-need proof for additional helpers, and — via the OP — candidate passport, medical report and school certificate. Ming Hwee prepares the Service Agreement, Service & Fee Schedule, Agency Fee form, Authorisation Form, Employer Particulars, Worker's Biodata (last page), Job Offer Form, Levy GIRO form (first-timers) and Worker's Employment History (if prior SG experience). Full list in [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- Phase 2 is strictly sequential — a step out of order can invalidate the MOM submission. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 2 · 2026-09-08)
- Never issue the air ticket before confirming the employer's pick-up availability; book flights only on written confirmation from both employer and OP/candidate. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §1 Phase 4 · 2026-09-08)
- The $35 fee and 4–6 week timeline are stated values that change — re-confirm against MOM before quoting. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §About NOTE · 2026-09-08)
