# Passport Renewal

## Definition
Renew the helper's home-country passport while she continues working in Singapore, handled through the relevant embassy. Process and forms differ by nationality. Also called "Passport Enable". (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
This is a runner-and-embassy service with no MOM permit change. Its documents and cadence are nationality-specific, and the Philippines path notably requires the worker's ORIGINAL passport and original signatures on embassy forms — a handling risk to track. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)

## Process
1. Capture the request; confirm nationality and current passport expiry. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
2. Confirm appointment availability with the runner (PH ~2 months ahead; Indo check dates first). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
3. Request documents per the nationality checklist (PH requires the original passport). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
4. Prepare embassy/agency forms; obtain original signatures (PH) or the completed & signed form (Indo). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
5. Submit to the embassy via the runner; track the appointment and processing. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
6. On completion, return the renewed passport to the helper/employer and update records. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)

## Documents
- **Philippines** — employer NRIC copy, worker's WP copy, worker's ORIGINAL passport; embassy docs (Contract, Undertaking Form, Annex A, Passport renewal form, OFW Info Sheet) completed and returned with original signature. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
- **Indonesia** — employer NRIC copy, worker's WP copy, worker's passport copy; Ming Hwee provides the Passport form to be completed and signed. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)

See [[Forms-and-documents-register]].

## Gotchas
- Original-document handling (PH): the worker's actual passport leaves her hands — track custody. This intersects with [[Passport-confiscation-protocol]]: a passport held for a legitimate embassy renewal is not confiscation, but the helper must know where it is and get it back. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
- Never quote a date before the runner confirms an appointment slot. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
- Myanmar is not covered by this service's document tables — the source gives PH and Indo only. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §4 · 2026-09-08)
