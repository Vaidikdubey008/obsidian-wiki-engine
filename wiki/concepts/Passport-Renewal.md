# Passport Renewal

## Definition
Renew the helper's home-country passport while she continues working in Singapore. Also called "Passport Enable". Handled through the relevant embassy; the process and forms differ by nationality. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §4. Passport Renewal · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
It is embassy-gated and runner-dependent: the constraint is appointment availability, not Ming Hwee's own processing. The whole service is bounded by when the embassy can see the helper, so lead-time advice must be confirmed with the runner before it is given.

## Full process
(src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §4. Passport Renewal · 2026-09-08)

1. Capture the request; confirm nationality and current passport expiry.
2. Confirm appointment availability with the runner (PH: advise ~2 months ahead; Indo: check dates before advising).
3. Request documents per the nationality checklist (PH requires the ORIGINAL passport).
4. Prepare the embassy/agency forms and obtain the required original signatures (PH) or the completed & signed form (Indo).
5. Submit to the embassy via the runner; track the appointment and processing.
6. On completion, return the renewed passport to the helper/employer and update records.

## Documents
- **Philippines** — requested: employer's NRIC copy, worker's WP copy, worker's ORIGINAL passport. Embassy docs completed and returned with original signature: Contract, Undertaking Form, Annex A, Passport renewal form, OFW Info Sheet.
- **Indonesia** — requested: employer's NRIC copy, worker's WP copy, worker's passport copy. Ming Hwee provides: Passport form (completed and signed).

See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §4. Passport Renewal · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- The Philippines route needs the helper's ORIGINAL passport in hand and original (wet) signatures — not copies or e-signatures.
- Never quote a lead time without confirming the runner's appointment availability first; the document makes this explicit for both nationalities.
- Only PH and Indonesia are documented. Myanmar is silent — treat Myanmar passport renewal as an open question, not an assumed-identical flow.
