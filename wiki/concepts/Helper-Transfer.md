# Helper Transfer

## Definition
An existing helper, already in Singapore on a valid Work Permit, moves from her current employer to a new one without going home. Ming Hwee assists with both outgoing transfers (releasing to a new employer) and incoming transfer hires, handling all MOM paperwork and employer matching. No embassy step and no overseas travel are involved; timeline 1–2 weeks from interview to deployment. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §7. Helper Transfer · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
This is the service most likely to be misclassified, and the source raises that to a hard rule. Because the helper is already in-country, treating a transfer as a [[New-Hiring]] sends her through overseas recruitment she does not need. It also depends on a fresh eligibility check for the new employer — see [[Employer-eligibility-screening]].

## Classification rule
The word "transfer" together with maid/helper/FDW/employer, "change employer", or any mention of an existing helper / existing Work Permit means TRANSFER — never New Hiring. Do not ask "hiring for the first time, or have you hired one?" for a transfer. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §7. Helper Transfer · 2026-09-08)

## Ticket capture — fields to collect
Current helper's name; current employer status (releasing / agreed?); Work Permit number and expiry date; reason for transfer; who is enquiring (current employer / new employer / the helper herself). Collect conversationally, one or two at a time. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §7. Helper Transfer · 2026-09-08)

## Full process
(src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §7. Helper Transfer · 2026-09-08)

1. Confirm in one line that this is a transfer of an existing helper (not a new hire).
2. Capture the ticket fields above.
3. Raise a Transfer ticket with all fields and hand to sales.
4. Matching (if the new employer needs a candidate) or confirmation (if the parties already agreed); facilitate an interview where needed.
5. New-employer eligibility check and e-authorisation via Singpass.
6. Complete the transfer agreement (cost-sharing between current and new employer) and the new employer's document set.
7. Submit the transfer / new WP application to MOM; on IPA, arrange the security bond and insurance under the new employer.
8. Issue the new WP; complete SIP if applicable; handover pack and case closure. No embassy or travel step.

## Documents
Requested: current helper's WP details (number & expiry), current employer release/agreement, new employer NRIC/IC copy, new employer income proof (Income Tax Assessment/Declaration; foreign employer: EP/S Pass + passport, or company letter + tenancy agreement). Prepared: transfer agreement between current & new employer (cost-sharing; retained 1 year and furnished to MOM on request), Authorisation Form (new employer, via Singpass), Employer Particulars form (new employer), Job Offer Form, fresh Employment Contract, Safety Agreement and Rest-Day form for the new employer. See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base-v1.md §7. Helper Transfer · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- Do not quote transfer pricing or process ad hoc — the source says raise the ticket and hand to sales.
- Eligibility is assessed against the NEW employer, from scratch — a transfer is not exempt from the seven-criteria check. See [[Employer-eligibility-screening]].
- The transfer (cost-sharing) agreement must be retained for 1 year and produced to MOM on request — a record-retention obligation, see [[Forms-and-documents-register]] and [[EA-licence-compliance]].
- A "transfer candidate already in SG" inside [[Direct-Hiring]] is a different situation (employer names the candidate); don't conflate the two.
