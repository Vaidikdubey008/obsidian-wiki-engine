# Helper Transfer

## Definition
An existing helper, already in Singapore on a valid Work Permit, moves from her current employer to a new one without going home. Ming Hwee assists with both outgoing transfers (releasing to a new employer) and incoming transfer hires, handling all MOM paperwork and employer matching. No embassy step and no overseas travel are involved. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)

## Why it matters here
Transfer carries an explicit classification rule that the chatbot and staff must not get wrong: the word "transfer" together with maid/helper/FDW/employer, "change employer", or any mention of an existing helper / existing Work Permit means TRANSFER — never [[New-Hiring]]. Do not ask "hiring for the first time, or have you hired one?" for a transfer. Misclassifying sends the case down a 4–6 week overseas flow instead of a 1–2 week in-SG one. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 CLASSIFICATION RULE · 2026-09-08)

## Quick reference
- Trigger: "transfer helper/maid", "change employer", "take over a transfer maid", "my helper wants to transfer". (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
- Flow type: ticket-capture → raise Transfer ticket → hand to sales (do not quote transfer pricing or process ad hoc). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
- Timeline: 1–2 weeks from interview to deployment. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
- Embassy / travel: none — the helper stays in Singapore. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
- Mandatory: new employer eligibility, e-authorisation, MOM transfer application, IPA, bond & insurance, SIP if applicable. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)

## Ticket capture fields
Current helper's name; current employer status (releasing / agreed?); WP number and expiry; reason for transfer; who is enquiring (current employer / new employer / the helper herself). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)

## Process
1. Confirm in one line that this is a transfer of an existing helper (not a new hire). (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
2. Capture the ticket fields conversationally, one or two at a time. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
3. Raise a Transfer ticket with all fields and hand to sales. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
4. Matching (if the new employer needs a candidate) / confirmation (if the parties already agreed); facilitate an interview where needed. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
5. New employer eligibility check and e-authorisation via Singpass. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
6. Complete the transfer agreement (cost-sharing between current and new employer) and the new employer's document set. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
7. Submit the transfer / new WP application to MOM; on IPA, arrange the bond and insurance under the new employer. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
8. Issue the new WP; complete SIP if applicable; handover pack and case closure. No embassy or travel step. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)

## Documents
Employer/client supplies the current helper's WP details, the current employer's release/agreement, the new employer's NRIC and income proof (or EP/S Pass + company letter + tenancy for foreign employers). Ming Hwee prepares the Transfer agreement (cost-sharing; retained 1 year and furnished to MOM on request), the new employer's Authorisation Form and Employer Particulars, the Job Offer Form, and a fresh Employment Contract, Safety Agreement and Rest-Day form for the new employer. See [[Forms-and-documents-register]]. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)

## Gotchas
- The classification rule is the whole game — never route a transfer through New Hiring's first-time-employer questions. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 CLASSIFICATION RULE · 2026-09-08)
- Do not quote transfer pricing or process ad hoc — always hand to sales. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
- The transfer agreement is a retained record (1 year, produced to MOM on request) — this ties into [[EA-licence-compliance]] document retention. (src: raw/minghwee-services-knowledge-base.md §7 · 2026-09-08)
