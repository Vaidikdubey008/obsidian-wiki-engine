# Module 27 — Helper Rights (Simple English)

## What this is
The helper-facing rights module, written at Grade 6 reading level so helpers from any source country can read it directly. The chatbot serves this content to helper users, optionally translated. It covers salary, placement loans, rest days, passport, food, accommodation, phone, safety routing, and the marriage/pregnancy work-permit rules.

## Key claims
- Salary must be paid monthly, in full, by the contract date or within 7 days. Deductions for breakages, food, or anything not in the contract are not permitted. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.1 · 2026-09-01)
- Unpaid salary escalates: tell the employer once, then Ming Hwee, then MOM at 1800-339-5505. Recovering unpaid salary is described as one of the main things MOM does. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.1 · 2026-09-01)
- A placement loan is money taken from the first months of salary to repay a source-country agency. The module calls it "the biggest money question in your first year." (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.1a · 2026-09-01)
- A helper has the right to a copy of the loan paper she signed in a language she understands, to know her balance at any time, and to have full salary resume immediately once the loan is finished. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.1a · 2026-09-01)
- "The loan is separate from your rights. Owing money does NOT mean you must accept bad treatment, no rest day, or no food." (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.1a · 2026-09-01)
- All placement loan figures — creditor, typical total, monthly deduction, number of months, treatment on transfer or early repatriation — remain unfilled placeholders. Until supplied and verified, the bot must route all loan questions to a human. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.1a · 2026-09-01)
- Two rest day rules apply. Rule 1: one full day off every week, which may be exchanged for extra pay by agreement. Rule 2: at least one day off every month that must be actually taken and cannot be exchanged for money. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.2 · 2026-09-01)
- The monthly non-compensable rest day rule started in January 2023 and is law; an employer can be punished for not following it. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.2 · 2026-09-01)
- Paying weekly in lieu so that a helper never gets a real day off is against the law "even if you agreed to it." (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.2 · 2026-09-01)
- The stated purpose of the monthly rule is that she can rest properly and meet people outside the house, because "the friends you make on your day off are the people who can help you." (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.2 · 2026-09-01)
- The passport belongs to the helper. Keeping it in a household safe is acceptable only if she knows the combination or has a key. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.3 · 2026-09-01)
- Entitlements also cover three meals a day and food she can eat, a mattress and private sleeping space with at least 7 hours of sleep, and her own phone with reasonable agreed usage limits. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.4–27.6 · 2026-09-01)
- Safety routing table: assault, serious illness or injury, confinement, and sexual contact route to 999/995. Salary, off-day, passport, food, accommodation, emotional support and repatriation requests route to Ming Hwee. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.7 · 2026-09-01)
- MOM helpline hours are given as Mon–Fri, 8:30am–5:30pm; the police non-emergency line 1800-255-0000 is 24 hours. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.7 · 2026-09-01)
- Prohibited conduct that can end a placement or lead to prosecution includes harming children, theft, unauthorised part-time work, and borrowing from unlicensed moneylenders. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.8 · 2026-09-01)
- Sexual contact or pressure by anyone in the household is a crime regardless of whether she said no, fought back, consented out of fear, or has already become pregnant. She will not lose her job for reporting it. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.8a · 2026-09-01)
- Work-permit rules on marriage and pregnancy are presented as government rules rather than moral judgements: MOM permission is required to marry a Singapore citizen or PR, a six-monthly health check includes a pregnancy test, and an unpermitted pregnancy usually results in permit cancellation. (src: raw/27-helper-rights-simple-english.md §27.8b · 2026-09-01)
