# Weekly digest prompt

---

Read `wiki/log.md` for the last 7 days plus any pages changed in that window.
Write a digest for the team, max 250 words:

- What we learned this week (3 bullets, most useful first)
- New conflicts opened, and what they block
- What changed in something we already believed
- One question worth answering next week, and where to find the answer

Plain prose. No headers beyond the four above. Assume the reader has not
opened the wiki.
